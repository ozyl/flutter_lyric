Initial lyric layout measurements -- 2026-10-11 (Asia/Shanghai)

Baseline: 8ad0a4c
Candidate: deferred active paragraphs, masks, row metrics and word bounds

Reproduction:
  flutter test test/benchmarks/lyric_load_benchmark.dart --reporter expanded

Copy the benchmark into the baseline checkout to compare. Run each revision
separately, using the same SDK and machine, without concurrent performance jobs.

Fixture: 400 x 600 viewport, default2 style, translated lyrics with six timed
words per line. Time includes layout, total height, active scroll position,
first active-word bounds and the first viewport's picture recording. Model
generation and disposal are excluded. Two warmups, then median of five loads.

                 baseline (ms)   candidate (ms)   reduction
  100 lines            17.71             6.86         61%
 1000 lines           133.00            52.20         61%
 5000 lines           634.22           249.41         61%

These are local Flutter test-engine CPU measurements, not profile-mode frame
times, GPU raster times, or end-to-end first-visible-frame latency. They are
one before/after pair of benchmark runs; device-specific results will vary.

Initial normal/translation measurements still scale with all lines to preserve
exact wrapping, content height, touch selection and arbitrary seek offsets.
Only the requested active line needs active layout; masks and word bounds are
cached on demand. Full viewport virtualization with estimated heights and
scroll-anchor correction is not implemented by this change.
